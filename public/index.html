Senior Living Truth Series · Custom business application

From seminar sign-up to operational clarity.

The Senior Living Truth Series needed more than a public-facing website. Its seminar program needed a reliable way to collect registrations, keep event information organized, and give the team a clear view of what was happening across sessions.

We built a custom Django application with registration workflows, admin tooling, reporting views, and an API/webhook connection into n8n so the right information could move into the rest of the operation automatically.
